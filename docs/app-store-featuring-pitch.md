# App Store Featuring Pitch

> Skickas via Apple Developer → **Promote your app on the App Store**.  
> Appen är **redan live** — pitcha vid **större uppdateringar** (t.ex. v2-nav, boendeschema, För dig-rutiner) eller när du har nya screenshots som visar tydligare värde. Apple svarar sällan, men kostar inget att skicka.  
> Skicka **6–8 veckor före** den release du vill att de ska lyfta (inte före första lansering).  
> Formuläret är på engelska — klistra in blocken nedan.

## The story (why we built it)

We built Min Stjärndag because mornings and bedtimes shouldn't be a daily power struggle. <!-- pragma: allowlist secret -->

As parents in Sweden, we saw families printing picture schedules, laminating cards, and taping them to walls — then re-printing every time the routine changed. We saw co-parents texting each other *"whose week is it?"* and *"did she take her medicine?"* We saw children with ADHD and autism thrive when they could see *what happens now* and *what comes next* — but struggle the moment that visual structure disappeared.

Min Stjärndag replaces the wall chart with a native iOS app: visual daily schedules with images and sub-steps, a child-friendly PIN login, and a treasure-chamber reward system (Skattkammaren) where stars unlock rewards the family chooses together. <!-- pragma: allowlist secret -->

We're a small, independent team (Papa Bravo AB) building specifically for Swedish families — but the product principles are universal: **predictability reduces stress, positive reinforcement beats nagging, and children deserve tools designed for them — not simplified adult apps.**

## What makes it unique

| Problem | Our approach |
|---------|--------------|
| Physical picture schedules don't sync between homes | Digital visual schedule + **custody calendar** (Week A/B) for alternating households |
| Nagging erodes parent-child relationships | **Star rewards** with child-chosen goals (restaurant visit, extra bedtime story) |
| Parents need different things than children | **Dual experience**: parent dashboard (status, planning) + independent child view (daily missions) |
| Getting started is overwhelming | **"For You" coaching tab** with one-tap routines: "Good mornings", "Calm evenings", "Independence" |
| Professionals need visibility | Secure share links for preschool, school, and therapists — no account required for viewer |

## Design & craft

- **Child-first information architecture:** three worlds (Today · Treasure Chamber · Family) — not a dumbed-down parent app
- **Visual sub-steps:** break "homework" into "get materials → work focused → clean up" — essential for neurodivergent children
- **Dark, calm parent UI** with warm gold accents; bright, friendly child UI
- **Accessibility-minded:** emoji + images + short labels; minimal navigation cognitive load
- **No ads, no dark patterns, GDPR/EU data residency**

---

## Apple technologies we use

- **Sign in with Apple** — frictionless parent onboarding
- **Native iOS app** via Capacitor with Apple Sign In (including iPad presentation-context fix for App Review)
- **APNs push notifications** — opt-in reminders for parents
- **In-App Purchase** via RevenueCat (family subscription model)
- **Human Interface Guidelines:** native tab bar, system fonts, respect for Dynamic Type where applicable
- **Privacy:** no third-party analytics without consent; child PIN gate for parent settings

## Tips before submitting

1. Replace `[Adjust date]` with your actual release window
2. Attach your **best 3 screenshots** (child daily view, treasure chamber, routines) if the form allows uploads
3. Mention **indie developer** explicitly — Apple features independent teams with niche, high-craft apps
4. Submit in **English** even though the app is Swedish-localized
5. One pitch per major milestone — don't spam; a big v2 launch is a good hook
