Your privacy in My Starday

For children and parents · review-notice version 0.1

Pending external review This page is written in plain language for families. It is not final legal text. A fuller privacy notice for parents is linked below.

My Starday helps with everyday routines — like brushing teeth, getting dressed, and earning stars. We keep only what we need to make that work. We do not sell your information, and we do not use your routines to show you adverts.

Parents and carers: you can read this page aloud to your child. The words are meant for children around 6–12, but the details are true for everyone in the family.

Who sets things up?

A grown-up in your family — usually a parent or carer — creates the My Starday account. They choose your activities, your schedule, and your rewards. You get to use the app with your own PIN.

How do you sign in?

Children sign in with a PIN (a short secret number), not with an email address. Your PIN helps keep your account yours. If you forget it, ask your grown-up for help.

What do we remember about you?

We keep things that help the app work for you:

  • Your first name or nickname — so the app can greet you
  • An emoji you pick — so you can recognise your profile
  • Your schedule — the activities for your day
  • Your stars — what you have earned and spent

We do not ask for your full name, your address, your phone number, or other details that would identify you outside the app. We do not collect health information about you.

Why do we keep schedules and stars?

So you can see what to do next, tick things off when you are done, and save up stars for rewards. That is the whole point of My Starday — to make mornings and evenings a little calmer and clearer.

Your grown-up is in charge

Your parent or carer can change your profile, update your schedule, or delete everything — the whole family account and all its data — at any time from their settings. When they delete the account, your information is removed for good.

What we never do

  • We do not sell information about children or families
  • We do not use your routine completions or star history to show personalised adverts
  • We do not share your day-to-day activity with advertising companies

Questions?

If something feels confusing or worrying, talk to your grown-up first. They can read the full privacy notice or contact us:
