A visual schedule board, or an app?

Many families start with a physical visual schedule board and Velcro pictures. It works — until the day moves between home, school, and grandparents. This is an honest comparison of a free printable and a living schedule in the app. Neither is “the right one” for every house.

Board or app

A board is concrete and visible in the kitchen. An app comes with you and changes in seconds. Choose from how your week actually moves.

When a board is the better fit

  • The child is home most of the day and the plan rarely changes.
  • They like moving real pictures with their hands.
  • You already have a board that the household uses.
  • An adult is usually beside it.

A board is not a lesser choice. It is tied to one room. A first-then board is often enough for a single transition.

When an app is the simpler fit

  • The day moves between home, school, and another house.
  • The plan changes often — clubs, siblings, one-off days.
  • You want rewards tied to the routine.
  • More than one adult needs to see the same schedule.
