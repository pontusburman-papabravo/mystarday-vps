How access to My Starday works

What is included today, how to get started, and how in-app purchase will work later — when billing is actually live. Prices are never shown as a web checkout on this page.

What is included?

  • Visual schedule and picture support for the child’s day
  • Star rewards via The Treasure Chamber
  • Routines with clear steps
  • Support for several children and adult collaboration
  • Sharing with educators where relevant

Read more about rewards or use for educators and therapists.

What does it cost right now?

Create an account if your country is open. A subscription is not required while public billing is off. When billing is live, prices are shown in the app (EUR in Ireland and Finland) before you buy — not as a web checkout here.

How will payment work later?

When public billing is on, purchases and subscriptions are handled in the app through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Exact prices and terms are shown there — not as a web purchase on this page.

Extra support — coming later

The Extra support package (the Seven Questions on the child’s NOW card, visual timer, and transition support) is in development and cannot be purchased yet. TEACCH-inspired printable cards are planned in a separate free resource library — not as a digital work board in the app.

How do I get started today?

Create a parent account if your country is open, follow the guide, and start building a schedule and rewards for your child.
