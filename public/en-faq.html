Frequently asked questions

Answers about My Starday for families. English product areas are in beta — some in-app text may still appear in Swedish.

What is My Starday?

A visual daily schedule with star rewards that helps children follow routines calmly.

What does it cost?

Early families may qualify for lifetime free access. See pricing for current details.

How does my child log in?

You create a child profile in the parent app. The child logs in with their name and PIN.

Is it safe for my child?

No ads, no social features, no tracking. Parents stay in control.
