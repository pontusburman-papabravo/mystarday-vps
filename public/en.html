How it works

How My Starday works

Three simple parts that help your child understand the day — and make it easier for you to stop nagging.

1

You build your child's routine

Add activities in the right order, adapt for morning, evening, or weekday, and create routines that fit your child.

2

Your child sees what happens next

Your child follows their schedule in their own view — one step at a time, what is done, what comes next, and how much is left.

3

Completed steps earn stars for the Treasure Chamber

When activities are completed, your child collects stars and works toward rewards you choose together. Progress becomes visible and motivation lasts longer.

Reward system

The Treasure Chamber — motivation your child can see

For many children, it is not enough to "just know" something is good. They need to see that they are moving forward. The Treasure Chamber makes progress visible and helps your child work toward rewards you choose together.

  • ⭐
    Stars make progress visibleWhen activities are completed, your child earns stars. That makes daily life more concrete.
  • 🎁
    You choose the rewards togetherSmall, everyday rewards — extra reading time, a game break, movie night, or something bigger to save toward.
  • 💚
    No punishment when the day is not perfectMissed activities do not reset your child. The focus is on motivation over time — not guilt or punishment.

Who it is for

For families who need a bit more structure in daily life

My Starday works especially well for children who thrive on clear steps, visual support, routines, and positive reinforcement — including children with ADHD, autism, or a need for visual schedules and structured pedagogy.

🎯

ADHD / hard to get started

When your child wants to but struggles to start, stay focused, or move to the next step.

🧩

Autism / need for predictability

When transitions, routine changes, or uncertainty create stress.

🖼️

Visual support and structured teaching

When your child understands visual steps better than repeated verbal reminders.

🌅

Morning and evening routines

When daily life gets stuck on the same steps again and again.

👨‍👩‍👧

Several adults around the child

When you need to share responsibility, see the same schedule, and work in the same direction.

✨

Children who need more motivation over time

When ordinary reward systems do not last or become too hard to follow up on.

That does not mean things have to be hard for the app to fit. My Starday is for families who want daily life to be clearer, calmer, and more independent — wherever you start.
