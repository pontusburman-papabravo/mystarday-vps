# 00B — Product Taste

**Authority:** Premium vs cheap — what we refuse to ship

---

## Bar

Nintendo fairness · Apple polish · Pixar warmth · Scandinavian children's book craft.

---

## Visual

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Handcrafted Nordic warmth | Stock art, generic SaaS gray |
| Soft ink, oak, morning light | Neon clutter, mixed illustration styles |
| Readable child typography | Dense admin patterns on child surfaces |
| Tokens: gold `#F5A623`, navy `#1B2340` | Magic hex outside design system |

---

## Motion

- Celebrations **≤2000ms**, skippable
- Routine path: perceived interactive **<200ms**
- Prefer transform/opacity — 60fps on mid-range Android
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`

---

## Copy (Swedish)

- Warm, short, capable child voice
- Parent copy: relief, not lecture
- Stars after accomplishment: *"Du klarade det!"* before counts

---

## Cheap list (never ship)

Login bonuses · loot boxes · guilt streaks · sibling leaderboards · paywalled pet · empty post-register home · triple coach · analytics-first Hem · developer-default UI
