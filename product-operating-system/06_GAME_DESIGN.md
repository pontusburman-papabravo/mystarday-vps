# 06 — Game Design

**Rule namespace:** G-01–G-08

---

## North star

> Play is the reward. Reality is the goal. **World is the destination — stars are fuel.**

---

## Motivation stack (four layers)

1. **Reality** — real routine completion  
2. **Progress** — visible forward motion  
3. **Identity** — "I am capable"  
4. **Discovery** — world delight (requires Layer 1)

PCB seven-layer stack in `.ai/product/PRODUCT_CONTENT_BIBLE.md` is creative depth — this four-layer stack is **engineering enforcement**.
