# 07 — Rewards & Stars

**Rule namespace:** R-01–R-06

---

## Principles

- Stars earned from **real completion** — never login, never purchase
- Lifetime stars **monotonic** (R-06) — no silent deduction
- Redemptions atomic server-side
- Skattkammaren bridges to **offline treats** parents approve

---

## Rules

| ID | Rule |
|----|------|
| R-01 | Stars require completed activity |
| R-02 | Stars not purchasable (IAP is subscription only) |
| R-03 | Parent configures rewards — child redeems |
| R-04 | No gambling metaphors |
| R-05 | Celebration after reality, not before |
| R-06 | Lifetime stars never decrease without explicit audit |

---

## Forbidden

Star packs · pay-to-skip progression · client-only balance
